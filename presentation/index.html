The insight
People don't need an AI that predicts who wins.
They need one that helps them prepare.

And an AI used for legal preparation is only useful if it can be trusted: every claim must point to where it came from.

This is the turning point of the talk. Prediction is unreliable and risky; preparation is valuable and checkable.

Architecture

A mobile app, a verifying backend, and a legal memory.

Three key decisions: (1) the LLM sits behind one adapter, so the provider can be swapped (free Gemini tier today, a local model tomorrow) and no API key is ever in the app; (2) one database for everything, including vectors, which means fewer moving parts; (3) heavy work runs in the background, so the app stays responsive.
Step 1 · Reading

Turning a phone photo into text, without inventing a word.

RECEIPT
Date: 15/03/2023

Received from Meera Iyer a sum of Rs. 5,50,000/- by cheque no. 004512 towards advance for the sale of plot Survey No. 123/A, Village Wadgaon, Pune.

Received by: Ravi Kulkarni

We benchmarked two OCR engines on the same scans. The winner:

Tesseract: Hindi key values on clean & phone-photo scans100%
Speed: Tesseract 0.5 s/page vs PaddleOCR ~26 s/page~40× faster
Memory: ~0.1 GB vs up to ~15 GB on a 16 GB laptopruns anywhere
Technical points: only pages without selectable text are OCR'd; OCR text is stored exactly as read, never "corrected" by AI; every page keeps a confidence score; low-confidence pages are flagged; a noisy page is automatically re-read after denoising (on degraded scans, key values went from 36% to 50% in English and from 20% to 50% in Hindi). We chose Tesseract over PaddleOCR by measurement: PaddleOCR's English model couldn't read Hindi at all, its Hindi model got 70% vs Tesseract's 100%, and it needed about 15 GB of RAM.
Step 2 · Understanding

Hybrid NLP: precise rules + a language model.

This Agreement to Sell is made on 15 March 2023 between Ravi Kulkarni (the SELLER) and Meera Iyer. The plot bearing Survey No. 123/4 is sold for a total sale consideration of Rs. 45,00,000/-, of which an advance of Rs. 5,00,000/- is paid. … सर्वे नंबर १२३/४ … रुपये ५ लाख …
amountsdatesproperty idspartieslegal terms

Rules (deterministic)

Indian formats: lakh/crore, ₹/Rs/रुपये, Devanagari digits, survey/khasra numbers, Hindi month names. Can't invent anything.

Language model (structured)

Reads meaning: who is the seller, which amount is the advance, what each clause says. Output must fit a strict schema.

Cross-check

Found by both → corroborated. Found only by the rules → "possibly missed" flag. Nothing silently disappears.

This is the heart of "NLP" in our project: information extraction combining rule-based extraction (regex grammars for Indian legal formats) with LLM structured extraction, then agreement checking. Rules give precision and guarantees; the model gives understanding.
Our core idea

The model proposes. The code verifies.

💬"The total price is Rs. 45,00,000." quote found on page 1✓ KEPT
💬"The advance was Rs. 7,00,000." ₹7,00,000 appears nowhere in the document✗ DROPPED
💬"You will definitely win this case." advice / outcome claim✗ DROPPED
📄Source "receipt.pdf, page 1" page exists, amount is on it✓ VERIFIED
📄Source "receipt.pdf, page 9" page doesn't exist✗ REMOVED
⚖️Judgment cited in chat was actually retrieved in this conversation✓ LINKED
Five layers: (1) strict output schemas; (2) quotes must exist on the cited page; (3) any amount, date or survey number in an AI statement must exist in the document, or the statement is dropped; (4) advice and outcome language is filtered; (5) citations to judgments or pages are checked against what was actually retrieved or uploaded. Unverified items are shown as unverified, not hidden.

Responsible AI

Helpful, but never harmful.

Refuses

Forging or back-dating documents, stamp-duty evasion, benami deals, bribery, threats, illegal eviction, digging up someone's personal details. Offers the lawful route instead.

Protects

If someone may be in danger: 112 police 181 women 14416 Tele-MANAS 15100 NALSA

Stays fair

No stereotyping by caste, religion, gender or community. Neutral words ("did not refund", not "fraudster"). No guaranteed outcomes.

Red-team suite: 17 cases (10 must-refuse, 2 people at risk, 2 bias/jailbreak, 3 legitimate questions that must not be refused), so we also catch an over-cautious assistant.

Mention the controls: a model that refuses everything would score perfectly on "refusal" but be useless, so legitimate questions are part of the test. The rules apply to every prompt that talks to users, plus a safe fallback if the provider blocks a reply.

Evaluation changed the product

Four problems no demo would have shown.

रुपये 5 लाख read as ₹5

The Hindi word for lakh wasn't understood, causing false "amount conflict" alarms (precision 96%). Fixed → 100%.

Survey no. १२३/४ vanished

Devanagari identifiers were erased during comparison, so real differences went unnoticed. Fixed and tested.

"5,50,000" became "5.50,000"

OCR read a comma as a dot, turning ₹5.5 lakh into ₹5.5. The parser now understands this OCR slip.

242 of 245 judgments skipped

The court dataset's language field was empty for new 2026 judgments. Our ingester now checks the actual file, not the flag.

This slide shows engineering maturity: measurement finds real problems. All four are fixed, each with a regression test.
